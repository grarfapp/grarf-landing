1. Overview

GRARF is a sports information service and Chrome browser extension that helps users discover sports games, scores, news, and related content.

GRARF is designed to operate without collecting personal information from extension users. Users do not need to create an account or provide personal information to use the extension's core features.

This Privacy Policy describes the information handling practices associated with the GRARF Chrome extension and its supporting services.

2. Information We Collect

We do not intentionally collect personal information from users through the GRARF Chrome extension. Specifically, GRARF does not ask users to provide their names, email addresses, telephone numbers, home addresses, or account credentials to use the extension.

We do not use the extension to build profiles of individual users, track their browsing activity across websites, or collect their browsing history for advertising or analytics.

The extension retrieves sports information from GRARF's supporting services. Like other internet services, these services and their infrastructure providers may process technical information associated with network requests, potentially including IP addresses, request timestamps, and standard security or diagnostic logs. Whether such information is recorded and how long it is retained depend on the relevant service and infrastructure configuration.

We do not intentionally use such technical information to identify individual users, build personal profiles, or track their browsing activity.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The extension may use browser storage or other local mechanisms as necessary to support its functionality and preferences.

We do not use the extension's local storage to build a personal profile of you or to transmit your browsing history to GRARF.

Any information stored locally by the extension remains subject to the browser's storage behavior and settings. Uninstalling the extension may remove extension-associated local data according to Chrome's behavior.

4. How Information Is Used

GRARF uses information processed in connection with the extension only as necessary to provide and maintain its intended functionality.

This includes:

  • Retrieving and displaying sports schedules, scores, game information, news, and related content.
  • Delivering requests between the extension and GRARF's supporting services.
  • Maintaining service availability, performance, and reliability.
  • Diagnosing technical errors and protecting services against abuse or security threats.

We do not sell personal information collected through the extension. We do not use personal information collected through the extension for targeted, personalized, or interest-based advertising.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

GRARF does not sell or rent users' personal information.

Technical information associated with network requests may be processed by hosting, infrastructure, security, or other service providers when necessary to operate and protect GRARF's services. Those providers may process information under their own applicable terms and privacy practices.

The extension may provide links to third-party websites, publishers, sports services, and other external destinations. When you choose to visit an external destination, that third party may receive information such as your IP address and browser request information and may collect additional information under its own privacy policy. GRARF does not control the independent data practices of those external services.

We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or integrity of GRARF, its users, or others.

6. Advertising and Sale of Information

GRARF does not sell users' personal information.

GRARF does not use personal information collected through the extension to create advertising profiles or deliver personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertisements.

If GRARF's business or data practices change in the future, this Privacy Policy and any applicable disclosures will be updated before the changed practices are introduced, as required by applicable law and platform policies.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

GRARF does not maintain user accounts or intentionally maintain a database of personal profiles collected through the extension.

Technical records that may be generated by supporting services or their infrastructure providers may be retained for operational, security, troubleshooting, or legal purposes in accordance with the applicable service configuration, retention practices, and legal obligations.

Because GRARF does not intentionally collect personal information through the extension for user profiles, there is generally no GRARF user profile for a user to access, export, or delete. If you have a privacy question or believe information associated with you has been processed by a GRARF service, contact avery@grarf.com so we can review the request.

Information stored by third-party websites or services is governed by their own policies and must be addressed with those providers where appropriate.

8. Security

GRARF takes reasonable measures designed to protect the services supporting the extension against unauthorized access, misuse, and disruption.

Connections to GRARF services use HTTPS where supported. No method of internet transmission, electronic storage, or security control can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The GRARF Chrome extension is not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the extension.

If you believe that personal information has been submitted to GRARF in a manner inconsistent with this policy, contact avery@grarf.com.

10. Your Choices

You can stop using the extension at any time by disabling or uninstalling it through Chrome.

You can choose whether to open links to external websites. Those websites operate independently and may have their own privacy controls, cookies, and data collection practices.

For questions about information handling by GRARF, contact avery@grarf.com.

13. Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

GRARF will handle any user data it accesses or processes in accordance with the applicable Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies, including the User Data Policy and its Limited Use requirements, where applicable.

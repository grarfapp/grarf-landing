• /live-whip-around_manual

    LIVE WHIP AROUND

    GRARF monitors every game and takes you to the biggest moments live as they happen.

    Manual Whip Around notifies you when an important moment is happening elsewhere and lets you decide whether to switch.

    Automatic Whip Around automatically switches to the most important live event as it unfolds.

  • /sportscape

    SPORTSCAPE

    GRARF organizes the sports internet so you can catch up on a whole day of action in minutes.

    For every game, get the 5-second summary, key moments, highlights, news articles, social media reactions, analysis, and everything else that mattered—all in one place.

  • /livetracker

    LIVETRACKER

    GRARF shows you everything that's happening across the sports world in real time.

    LiveTracker is a live feed of the most important moments across all leagues—home runs, goals, touchdowns, knockouts, lead changes, injuries, milestones, breaking news, and other important developments as they happen.

    Click any event to watch the clip, go to the live stream, or open the game workspace for a deeper dive.
